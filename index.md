I'm an AI safety researcher and founder working on AI for epistemics and coordination. I'm an [Astra Fellow](https://constellation.org/programs/astra) on the strategy and governance stream, working out where the field most needs accelerating to ensure a better future, and I consult on enterprise AI transformation through [Proferra](https://proferra.org/), with a focus on better organisational coordination.

Previously: contracted with [Forethought](https://www.forethought.org/) as a founder in residence, scoping the field of AI for epistemics and coordination; [Talos](https://www.talosnetwork.org/) Policy Leaders Fellow (EU AI governance); [Apart](https://apartresearch.com/) Studio Fellow (sycophancy evals); facilitator on BlueDot's [AGI Strategy course](https://bluedot.org/courses/agi-strategy). Before AI safety I founded and sold an e-commerce startup while at secondary school, ran an education company in Hong Kong, and worked as an AI engineer building tools for enterprises and political candidates. I read archaeology and anthropology at Oxford, where I dug on a Roman Iberian excavation at [Castelo de Cuncos](https://www.castelodecuncosproject.com/) and wrote my dissertation on human cognitive enhancement.
