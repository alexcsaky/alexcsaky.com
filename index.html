Alex Csaky

alexcsaky@gmail.com · GitHub · LinkedIn · Substack

I'm an AI safety researcher and founder working on AI for epistemics and coordination. I'm an Astra Fellow on the strategy and governance stream, working out where the field most needs accelerating to ensure a better future, and I consult on enterprise AI transformation through Proferra, with a focus on better organisational coordination.

Previously: contracted with Forethought as a founder in residence, scoping the field of AI for epistemics and coordination; Talos Policy Leaders Fellow (EU AI governance); Apart Studio Fellow (sycophancy evals); facilitator on BlueDot's AGI Strategy course. Before AI safety I founded and sold an e-commerce startup while at secondary school, ran an education company in Hong Kong, and worked as an AI engineer building tools for enterprises and political candidates. I read archaeology and anthropology at Oxford, where I dug on a Roman Iberian excavation at Castelo de Cuncos and wrote my dissertation on human cognitive enhancement.
